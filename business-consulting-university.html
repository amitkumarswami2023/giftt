Programme Overview

01

Business consulting is being transformed by AI, faster decision cycles, and increasingly complex business environments.

02

Traditional consulting relied heavily on research, frameworks, and analysis. Today’s consultants must combine structured problem-solving, business acumen, client influence, financial literacy, and AI-enabled execution.

03

This programme prepares participants to think, communicate, and deliver like high-performing consultants while leveraging AI as a force multiplier.

Key Learning Outcomes

By the end of this programme, participants will be able to:

Think and solve problems like a consultant using structured frameworks

Use AI tools to accelerate research, analysis and presentations

Build consulting-style business recommendations and executive decks

Conduct market sizing, feasibility analysis and strategic assessments

Improve communication, stakeholder management and presentation skills

Prepare for consulting interviews, case discussions and guesstimates

Build a consulting-ready portfolio and AI toolkit

Who Should Attend

Designed for aspiring consultants, business professionals & leaders seeking strategic consulting capabilities

Fresh graduates looking to enter consulting or advisory roles

Early professionals wanting to move into strategy or business consulting

Young entrepreneurs and startup founders
