Curriculum Structure

Module 1: Foundations of AI-Powered Digital Marketing

Understand how digital marketing is evolving in the AI era and how modern marketers are building smarter growth systems.

What You Will Learn

  • Evolution of digital marketing and changing consumer behaviour
  • AI-driven customer journeys and marketing funnels
  • Building an AI-powered marketing tech stack
  • Content creation and storytelling using AI tools
  • Scalable content systems and workflow automation

Module 2: Performance Marketing & Growth Automation

Learn how to drive customer acquisition, conversions, and engagement using AI-powered marketing strategies.

What You Will Learn

  • AI-powered performance marketing and paid advertising
  • SEO, Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & AI search visibility
  • Marketing automation and lead nurturing systems
  • Customer journey design and omnichannel experiences
  • Conversion optimization and personalization strategies

Module 3: Data Analytics & Customer Intelligence

Develop the ability to make data-driven marketing decisions using analytics, dashboards, and predictive intelligence.

What You Will Learn

  • Marketing KPIs and performance measurement
  • Predictive marketing and customer intelligence
  • Customer segmentation and personalization at scale
  • Growth dashboards and reporting frameworks
  • Data-driven decision-making for marketing leaders

Module 4: AI Branding, Strategy & Leadership

Build future-ready leadership capabilities to lead digital transformation and AI-first marketing teams.

What You Will Learn

  • Brand positioning and differentiation in the AI era
  • Future consumer behaviour and digital trends
  • Leading AI-enabled marketing teams and workflows
  • Digital transformation and growth strategy
  • Capstone project with AI marketing systems and growth planning

Capstone Deliverables

Apply your learning through practical AI-powered marketing assets and strategic growth frameworks.

Deliverables

  • AI Marketing Stack
  • Growth Dashboard
  • Automation Blueprint
  • AI Content Engine
  • 90-Day Growth Plan
