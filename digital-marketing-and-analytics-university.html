Programme Overview

01

Digital marketing is undergoing its biggest transformation since the rise of social media. Artificial Intelligence is redefining how brands discover customers, create content, optimize campaigns, automate workflows, and drive business growth.

02

Modern marketers are expected to combine creativity, analytical thinking, and AI-powered execution to deliver measurable business outcomes. Organizations today seek professionals who can leverage AI tools, understand customer journeys, optimize performance marketing, and make data-driven decisions at scale.

03

This programme prepares students and early professionals to become AI-enabled digital marketers and growth professionals. Participants will learn AI-powered content creation, performance marketing, search optimization, automation, analytics, branding, and growth leadership through hands-on workshops, live demonstrations, marketing simulations, and real-world industry case studies.

Key Learning Outcomes

By the end of this programme, participants will be able to:

Understand how AI is transforming marketing, search, and customer behaviour

Build AI-powered content creation and campaign execution systems

Use AI tools across content, research, SEO, automation, and analytics

Plan and execute performance marketing campaigns across Google and Meta ecosystems

Optimize brands for SEO, GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) and AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)

Build marketing automation and customer nurturing workflows

Measure campaign effectiveness using analytics, attribution, and dashboards

Develop future-ready digital marketing, growth, and leadership capabilities

Build an AI-powered marketing portfolio and professional brand

Who Should Attend

Designed for students, graduates, professionals, entrepreneurs, and aspiring marketing leaders

Final-year students aspiring for careers in Digital Marketing, Growth Marketing, Brand Management, and Performance Marketing

MBA, BBA, Engineering, and Business students

Fresh graduates seeking opportunities in marketing, advertising, media, and growth roles

Early professionals looking to transition into digital marketing or growth-focused careers

Entrepreneurs and startup founders building digital-first businesses

Freelancers and aspiring marketing consultants
