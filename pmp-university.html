Programme Overview

01

AI is fundamentally reshaping how organisations plan, execute, and govern projects. From predictive risk engines and AI-powered resource allocation to intelligent scheduling and automated reporting, project management is entering a new era of data-driven execution.

02

Today's project managers must go beyond traditional execution skills and combine strategic leadership with AI-augmented capabilities to improve forecasting, decision-making, stakeholder engagement, and delivery performance.

03

This executive programme equips professionals with PMBOK 7's 12 Principles, the 8 Performance Domains, AI-powered project management tools, and transformation leadership capabilities required to drive high-performance delivery in an AI-driven world.

Key Learning Outcomes

By the end of the programme, participants will be equipped with modern project management, AI-enabled delivery, and career acceleration capabilities.

Understand the 12 PMBOK 7 Principles and apply them to real project scenarios

Navigate the 8 Project Performance Domains including Stakeholders, Team, Planning, Delivery, Measurement and Uncertainty

Apply AI tools to automate scheduling, risk tracking and project reporting

Use proven PM frameworks including Agile, Scrum, Waterfall and Hybrid models

Build structured project documentation, charters and stakeholder communication plans

Leverage AI for predictive analytics, resource optimisation and project decision support

Prepare for CAPM, PMP and PMI-ACP certification pathways

Build a PM-ready portfolio and professional LinkedIn presence
