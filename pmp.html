Programme Overview

01

AI is fundamentally reshaping how organisations plan, execute, and govern projects. From predictive risk engines and AI-powered resource allocation to intelligent scheduling and automated reporting, project management is entering a new era of data-driven execution.

02

Today's project managers must go beyond traditional execution skills and combine strategic leadership with AI-augmented capabilities to improve forecasting, decision-making, stakeholder engagement, and delivery performance.

03

This executive programme equips professionals with PMBOK 7's 12 Principles, the 8 Performance Domains, AI-powered project management tools, and transformation leadership capabilities required to drive high-performance delivery in an AI-driven world.

Key Learning Outcomes

By the end of the programme, participants will be equipped to lead AI-enabled projects, build intelligent PMO systems, and drive enterprise transformation with confidence.

Apply the 12 PMBOK 7 Principles and 8 Performance Domains to lead high-stakes enterprise projects

Lead AI-augmented project delivery from planning through closure

Use AI tools to automate scheduling, risk tracking, reporting and stakeholder updates

Apply Agile, Waterfall and Hybrid PM methodologies in complex organisational environments

Build and manage AI-powered PMO workflows and governance structures

Leverage predictive analytics and real-time dashboards for data-driven project decisions

Lead change management and stakeholder alignment in AI transformation initiatives

Develop responsible AI practices and ethical governance for enterprise projects
